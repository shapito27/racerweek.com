Inspired by F1 in Malaysia, and by the week every year when Sepang had KL talking about nothing but racing.

You drive a small open-wheel race car down a three-lane city street at night. Kapcai bikes, hatchbacks, barriers and cone rows fill the lanes, and you swerve between them. The further you go, the faster it gets, and the city changes around you: from the neon of Bukit Bintang, through Chinatown, Little India and Merdeka Square, past the Twin Towers, all the way to the golden statue at Batu Caves.

## How to play

| | Phone | Computer |
|---|---|---|
| Change lane | Tap the left or right side | <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> |
| Retry | Tap | <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> |

One hit ends the run. Grab the glowing rings and squeeze past traffic for bonus points. Your best score is saved on your device.

## Street details

**Shop boards.** Kopitiam, mamak, banana leaf, cendol, flower garlands and gold. The kopitiam in Chinatown carries its name in Chinese, and the banana-leaf restaurant in Little India in Tamil.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/readme/img/street-boards.png" width="600" alt="Shop boards"></p>
